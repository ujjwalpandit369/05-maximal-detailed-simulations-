The 3-Torus Universe

This simulation provides a first-person, immersive view of what it would be like to live in a universe with the topology of a 3-torus. This is a universe that is finite, but has no boundaries or edges. The "hall of mirrors" or "infinite crystal" effect you see is a direct consequence of this topology. Light from a single object can reach you from many different directions after wrapping around the finite dimensions of the space, creating the illusion of an infinite, repeating lattice of objects.
