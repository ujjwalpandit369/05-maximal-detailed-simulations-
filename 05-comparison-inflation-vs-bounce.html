Head-to-Head Comparison

Feature Inflation Bounce
Origin Story A tiny patch of a pre-existing vacuum undergoes a phase transition, triggering exponential expansion. A previous contracting universe collapses, and quantum gravity effects cause a "bounce" into our expanding phase.
The Singularity Avoids the Big Bang singularity but still requires a beginning (the start of inflation), and may emerge from a prior singularity. Explicitly avoids the singularity, replacing it with a physical, albeit extreme, bounce mechanism. Potentially part of an eternal cycle.
Solving Puzzles Solves Horizon/Flatness problems by stretching a tiny, smooth patch of space to enormous size. Solves Horizon/Flatness problems by providing vast time during a slow contraction phase for the universe to smooth itself out.
Primordial Gravitational Waves Predicts a potentially detectable, nearly scale-invariant background of gravitational waves. A "smoking gun" signature. Predicts a much weaker background of gravitational waves, likely undetectable. The absence of a signal favors this model.
Non-Gaussianity Simple models predict very low levels of non-gaussianity in the CMB. Can generate specific, larger (but still small) non-gaussian signatures that could be a distinguishing feature.
Key Ingredient A hypothetical "inflaton" scalar field with a specific potential energy shape. An unknown mechanism of quantum gravity to mediate the bounce, often involving extra dimensions or brane collisions.
